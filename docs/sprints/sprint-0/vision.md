# Assist Club - Product Vision

Authentic sports memorabilia with a solidarity touch: join the club and assist for a goal.

## Vision Statement
Assist Club is an online platform for auctioning authentic sports memorabilia while supporting charitable causes and NGOs. This platform aims to make these exclusive sports items accessible through a trustworthy and transparent auction while assisting important causes and supporting sports clubs around the world.

## Problem
Fans and collectors may find it hard to verify the authenticity of sports memorabilia. Meanwhile, clubs need profitable ways to sell these items. 

## Target Users
- **Buyers**: sports fans and collectors interested in buying exclusive items while supporting causes;
- **Clubs**: sports clubs looking to sell exclusive items and raise funds;
- **Charitable organisations**: verified organizations that receive contributions from completed auctions.

## Proposed Solution
Assist Club provides a centralised platform where users can browse memorabilia and participate in timed auctions. Listings include evidence of authenticity, item details and information about the associated charitable contribution. Each item comes with a certificate of authenticity.

Clubs get to pick who to support and what percentage of the final sale goes to it. A small fixed percentage of each sale goes to the platform, our main source of revenue.

Joining authenticity, social impact and convenience: **everyone wins**!

## Values

Assist Club combines three core values:

- **Authenticity**: buyers ensure they’re getting authentic and exclusive items;
- **Social impact**: verified organisations earn contributions;
- **Convenience**: for buyers, clubs and organisations, all in the same platform.
